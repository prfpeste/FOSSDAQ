# BOM
- 1 x Arduino Nano 33 BLE Rev2 - ARD NANO 33BLE2
- 2 x Breadboard (perfboard), double-sided, 80 x 20 mm - LR-DS-28 (1x sensor terminal carrier, 1x strip for the 24 V bus terminal)
- 1 x DC/DC step-down converter LM2596S with display (Joy-IT) - DEBO DCDC DOWN 5
- 4 x Spring loaded terminal, 6-pin, 5 mm pin spacing - AST 025-06 (sensor terminals)
- 1 x Spring loaded terminal, 4-pin, 5 mm pin spacing - AST 025-04 (24 V bus input + pass-through)
- 1 x USB-A to Micro-USB-B data cable, 1 m (USB-C cable for the Nano R4)
- approx. 1 m stranded wire H05V-K 1 x 0.75 mm², red (+24 V / +5 V)
- approx. 1 m stranded wire H05V-K 1 x 0.75 mm², black (0 V)
- approx. 2 m stranded wire H05V-K 1 x 0.75 mm², green (sensor signals A0-A7)
- 3D printing PLA filament
- min. 15 x self-tapping Torx screws M2.3 x 6 mm (assortment set) - B0G6YNG9CW

Notes: Part designations follow the supplier product titles, so
they can be found directly together with the order codes. Wire colors are
freely selectable - recommended: red for +24 V/+5 V, black for 0 V and
green for signal wires (consistent across the whole system).
